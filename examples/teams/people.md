# People

Everyone who appears in a team, once. Team files link to people by email, written `Name <email>` (or `Name <swarmia:ID>` for someone without an email); the name is only for reading. Add a row here before adding someone to a team. Format and privacy rules: [README.md](README.md).

## Attributes

Columns with source `swarmia` are refreshed on every pull from Swarmia: change them in Swarmia. Columns with source `local` are yours. Allowed values, comma-separated, are checked; leave empty for free text.

| Attribute | Source | Allowed values |
|---|---|---|
| role | local | Engineer, Engineering manager |
| seniority | local | Mid, Senior, Staff |
