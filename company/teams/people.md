# People

Everyone who appears in a team, once. Team files refer to people by the name in this table (or `Name <email>` when two people share a name). Add a row before adding someone to a team. Format and privacy rules: [README.md](README.md).

## Attributes

Columns with source `swarmia` are refreshed on every pull from Swarmia: change them in Swarmia. Columns with source `local` are yours. Allowed values, comma-separated, are checked; leave empty for free text.
