# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good intent, critique ideas rather than people, and keep discussion on topic. Harassment and personal attacks are not tolerated.

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers at hello@swarmia.com. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments, close issues or block contributors who break this code.
